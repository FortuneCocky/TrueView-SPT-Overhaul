; Read by the mod, not the engine: shot on the run (staggerFall). The upright pull decides how many
; stumbling steps he takes before he goes down: 3.2 and he tumbles after 1-2 steps, 3.6 and up he
; staggers 4-6. Slower runs get the stiffer pull, so the faster he was going the likelier he goes down.
; Offline: 58% down at 3.5 m/s, about 90% at 5 m/s and over.
EuphoriaRun * {
  runStaggerSpeed                 3.5     ; m/s: hit moving at least this fast, he staggers on (slower: the shot above)
  runUprightSlow                  4.5     ; upright pull of the stagger at runStaggerSpeed
  runUprightFast                  3.6     ; ... at 5 m/s and over
}
